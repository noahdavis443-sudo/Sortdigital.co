FAQ

Questions people ask before starting.

What's included in the free audit?

We review your website, Google Business Profile, reviews, social media and ad tracking, give each a score out of 100, and list the top fixes in order of impact. It's yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. Every service can be bought on its own. Many clients start with a website or local SEO and add more later.

What does "AI-powered" actually mean for my business?

Practical things: a chatbot on your site that answers questions and books appointments at any hour, instant follow-up when a lead comes in, and AI-assisted content and ads that let us produce more for the same budget. A person on our team reviews everything before it goes out.

Can you build a custom AI tool for us?

Yes. We build chatbots, automations that connect the apps you already use, and AI features inside websites and apps. We scope each project on a call and send a fixed quote.

Is ad spend included in your prices?

No. You pay Google or Meta directly for ad spend, so you always see exactly where your money goes. Our fee covers setup, management and reporting.

How fast will I see something live?

Your first deliverable, such as an updated Google profile, a new website draft or your first social posts, goes live within 14 days of starting.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds, from contractors and clinics to restaurants, shops, professional services and trucking companies.
